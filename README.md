Modular is a sound design plugin developed under Frameworks, designed to give music producers detailed control over the tonal, textural, and spatial characteristics of audio. Its workflow centers on three primary sound-shaping dimensions: Texture, Color, and Space, allowing users to transform source material into more distinctive, expressive, and mix-ready sounds.
The plugin is built around a creative sound-processing workflow in which each control influences a different aspect of the sound's character, from its surface detail and harmonic qualities to its sense of depth and spatial placement.
Core processing dimensions
- Texture: Alters the perceived surface and complexity of a sound. Depending on the implemented processing, this can involve adding density, grit, noise, transient detail, or evolving tonal layers.
- Color: Shapes the spectral and harmonic character of the signal. This dimension can influence brightness, warmth, tonal balance, and harmonic saturation.
- Space: Changes the perception of depth, width, and ambience. Spatial processing can include reverberation, delay, stereo imaging, and early-reflection characteristics.
Signal-processing workflow
At a technical level, Modular can be described as an audio-processing environment that takes an incoming signal, applies user-controlled transformations, and outputs the processed result. Its effectiveness depends on how the processing stages interact with the source material and with one another.
A typical audio-effects workflow follows this structure:
1. Input stage: Receives audio from a track or bus in a digital audio workstation (DAW).
2. Parameter control: Translates user adjustments into processing parameters.
3. Digital signal processing (DSP): Applies the underlying algorithms to modify the incoming audio.
4. Output stage: Delivers the processed signal back to the DAW for further mixing, routing, or rendering.

Intended applications
Modular's sound-shaping approach is suited to a range of production tasks:
- Sound design and synthesis enhancement
- Tonal shaping and harmonic character development
- Layering and enriching samples
- Creating atmospheric depth and stereo dimension
- Processing drums, basses, synthesizers, and other audio sources
- Developing distinctive sounds for electronic music, hip-hop, cinematic production, and experimental audio
In summary: Modular is a sound-shaping tool built around the interaction of texture, tonal character, and spatial depth. Its purpose is to provide producers with a focused interface for manipulating the character and perception of audio rather than relying exclusively on conventional corrective processing.
